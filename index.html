For You

I don't really know where to start with this, because there are some people who slowly become a part of your life without you even realizing it.

And somehow, you became one of those people for me. Not because everything was perfect, and not because every moment was some huge unforgettable event.

It's actually the little things that stayed with me. The random conversations, the stupid jokes, the arguments over absolutely nothing, and all those moments where we somehow ended up laughing at things that probably weren't even funny.

I think that's what makes a friendship special. You don't always notice it happening. You just wake up one day and realize that talking to that person became part of your normal.

And honestly, I'm glad it happened.

We've had our weird moments. We've misunderstood each other sometimes. We've gone quiet. We've talked again. We've probably annoyed each other more times than either of us would like to admit.

But somehow, we're still here.

And that's something I genuinely appreciate.

You have this way of making ordinary moments feel like memories. Sometimes it's just a conversation, sometimes it's a game, sometimes it's literally just you being you.

I don't think you always realize how much your presence can mean to someone.

So I wanted this little surprise to be more than just a birthday message.

I wanted to make something that you could actually go through. Something with little challenges, memories, jokes, and things that remind you of the person you are.

Because you deserve something that feels like it was actually made for you.

I'm really grateful for everything we've shared so far.

And I'm even more curious about what comes next. 🌌

I've already made you wait long enough 😭

So I think we should stop talking about all this for now...

Because we're still not done yet.
