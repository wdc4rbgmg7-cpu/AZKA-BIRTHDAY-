For You

I was trying to think about what I should even write here because honestly there are so many things I could say and I don't even know where to start 😭

When I think about everything we've shared, I don't think it's one specific moment that stands out the most. It's all the random little things that happened without us even realizing how much we'd remember them.

Like how we'd play CODM for so long that we'd probably lose track of how many matches we'd played 😭 Sometimes we'd actually be trying to win and sometimes we'd just be doing random stuff and somehow still keep playing.

And then there were all those nights when we'd just talk and somehow the conversation would keep going. We'd start talking about one thing and somehow end up talking about something completely different. Sometimes we'd have serious conversations, sometimes we'd just be saying the most random things ever, and somehow we'd still have something else to talk about.

And obviously I can't forget how good you became at roasting me 😭 I genuinely don't know how you always managed to find something to say. I used to call you CarryMinati's student but honestly I think I was giving you too little credit. At this point you were probably his coach 💀

There were also all those times we watched movies and series together. We'd start something at the same time and just sit there watching it, then talk about it afterward. It sounds like such a normal thing when I say it like that, but those moments genuinely mean a lot to me.

And I really appreciated the times when things weren't just jokes and games too. There were times I'd tell you about something going on with people around me or something that was bothering me, and you'd actually give me advice. Some of the things you told me genuinely helped me look at situations differently. You didn't always just tell me what I wanted to hear either, and I think that's one of the things I appreciated about you.

I don't think there was ever one specific moment where you suddenly became important to me. It just happened naturally. We talked more, played more, joked more, watched things together, shared random things with each other, and somehow you just got really, really close to me.

Like genuinely close.

I've had people around me before. I've had friends and I've met a lot of different people, but no one really got that close to me.

And I don't even think I realized it while it was happening.

I was just enjoying the conversations, the games, the jokes, the random arguments, the roasting, the movies, everything.

And I think that's what makes all of it special.

We weren't trying to make memories.

We were just having fun.

We were just talking.

We were just being ourselves.

And somehow all those normal moments became things that I genuinely care about.

I don't think you always realize how much the little things can mean to someone. A conversation can just feel like a normal conversation while you're having it, but sometimes you realize that the person you're talking to has become a really important part of your life.

And honestly, that's how I feel about everything we've shared.

You really do mean a lot to me.

Not because of one specific thing you did or because of one particular moment. I mean it because of everything together.

All the games.

All the random conversations.

All the late nights.

All the roasting 😭

All the movies and series.

All the advice.

All the stupid little moments that probably seemed completely normal at the time.

They all became memories that I'm genuinely grateful for.

And I don't want this to sound like I'm talking about something that's finished or something that belongs only in the past.

I just wanted you to know that all those moments became a part of how much I appreciate having you in my life.

And honestly, I'm really glad I got to experience all of it with you.

There are still so many things ahead of us that we haven't even experienced yet.

So yeah...

I'm really grateful for everything we've shared so far.

And I'm even more curious about what comes next. 🌌

I've already made you wait long enough 😭

So I think we should stop talking about all this for now...

Because we're still not done yet.

Let's continue.
